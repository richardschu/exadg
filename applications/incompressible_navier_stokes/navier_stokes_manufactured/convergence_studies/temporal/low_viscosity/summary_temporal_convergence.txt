Observations:
- All 18 studies run through. The Chebyshev smoother is used for all multigrid
  levels, with the preconditioners updated in every time step. Earlier, the
  eigenvalue estimation broke down (NaN) on the continuous (float) cphMG levels;
  now ChebyshevSmoother uses deal.II's non-fused algorithms on continuous levels
  (AdditionalData::use_fused_operations = is_dg).
- BDF1, BDF2: rates 1 and 2 for all schemes and viscosity treatments; the
  errors of the three schemes agree to 2-3 digits (pressure correction BDF1
  velocity about x2 larger).
- BDF3:
  - coupled: rate 3 down to velocity ~3e-11, pressure ~5e-11.
  - dual_splitting: about 3 down to dt ~1/32, then first order from dt = 1/128
    (the splitting error, as in the paper).
  - pressure_correction: rate 3 down to dt = 1/128, unstable from dt = 1/256
    (errors O(1)) with pressure extrapolation order 2 (order_time_integrator - 1
    in this app), as for high viscosity.
- Explicit and implicit viscosity give the same errors to 2-3 digits; implicit
  viscosity costs about +15% in time for coupled, +5% for dual splitting and
  +30% to x2 for pressure correction.
